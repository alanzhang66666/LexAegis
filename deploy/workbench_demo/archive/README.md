algorithms and browser drivers. They are not imported or executed by the product,
build or test discovery. Local case strings, record IDs, private paths and temporary
URLs were removed. They cannot reproduce private evidence or grant authorization.

verification. The old drivers are historical references, not supported entrypoints.
